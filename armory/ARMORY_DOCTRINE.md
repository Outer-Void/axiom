Armory Doctrine

- Armory exists to discipline execution, not to perform destructive actions.
- Armory is Debian-first and operationally predictable.
- Armory maintains clear intent signaling: DISARMED or ENGAGED with a named weapon and profile.
- Only one weapon may be active at a time.
- Engagement must be explicit; default state is DISARMED.
- Safe fallback rule: if state is inconsistent or unavailable, default to safe and blocked.
- The runner is the single gateway for interception.
- v0.1 provides scaffolding only; no weapon may implement real-world effects.
